        Privacy

        The public site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, nothing stored on your device, no personal data and no tracking across other sites. Your map view and routes stay in your browser. Routes, place names, the Land Tirol elevation and nextmap's Sentinel-2 mosaics come from this site; the rest loads straight from its providers (basemap.at, AWS, OpenFreeMap, and Microsoft Planetary Computer or OpenStreetMap when you look outside Tyrol), which see your IP address as any website you visit does.
